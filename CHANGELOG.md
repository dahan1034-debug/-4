# Changelog

## 0.5.0 — Campaign progression and selectable aircraft
- Added a three-sortie campaign with SAM, interceptor, and combined objectives.
- Added mission completion and progression persistence.
- Added three airframes with distinct speed, acceleration, maneuverability, hull, and missile loadout.
- Added in-game airframe selection by buttons or 1/2/3 keys.
- Connected weapons to explicit live target metadata and made cannon fire ballistic.
- Made engine, armor, and missile upgrades affect aircraft gameplay.
- Improved HUD with aircraft, campaign objective, weapons and credits status.

## 0.4.0 — Multi-weapon combat and synthesized audio
- Added missile, cannon, and rocket weapon modes with procedural audio cues.
- Added radar scope and upgrade-panel interactions.

## 0.3.0 — Tactical awareness
- Added RWR, flare countermeasures, improved guidance, and interceptor behavior.

## 0.1.0 — Prototype
- Added the initial procedural Three.js flight-combat game.
