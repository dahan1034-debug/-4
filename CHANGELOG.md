# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — Tactical awareness and countermeasures
- Added a radar warning receiver with relative bearing, range, contacts, and inbound missile warning.
- Added limited flare inventory, visible decoy effects, and seeker diversion behavior.
- Improved missile homing to track live targets and cleaned up missile geometry/material resources.
- Added target selection and damage handling for SAM sites and interceptor aircraft.
- Added interceptor patrol/intercept/engage/disengage states and cooldown-based attacks.
- Corrected return-to-runway mission completion distance and persistent score updates.

## 0.1.0 — Initial prototype
- Added the modular Three.js browser flight game scaffold, procedural mountain terrain, aircraft controls, weapons, enemies, mission score, and local saves.
